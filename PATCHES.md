# Patches

Drive Player's modifications to libvlcjni and VLC in this build. `third-party/libvlc/patches/libvlcjni/` (4 patches)
is applied to libvlcjni, and `third-party/libvlc/patches/vlc/` (100 patches) to VLC after the patches libvlcjni itself
carries, all with `git am`. Each patch's message describes its change in full; the tables below say what each one
solved and what it changes, grouped by what they concern. Numbers missing from the series were removed or not adopted:
0010 and 0018 are in the tags of earlier versions, and 0020-0024, never built, in this repository's history.

| [`vlc/0099`](third-party/libvlc/patches/vlc/0099-audiotrack-count-the-output-latency-the-playback-head-leaves-out.patch) | The AudioTrack output's position, from the playback head, leaves out the latency past it (some 100 to 130 ms), and no delay was known before anything was written. | [`vlc/0097`](third-party/libvlc/patches/vlc/0097-wav-read-g726-in-frames-of-20-ms.patch) | The WAV demuxer read G.726 a block at a time, commonly one byte: dating 4000 frames a second, the audio output resampled to and fro and the tone came out off its pitch. | [`vlc/0103`](third-party/libvlc/patches/vlc/0103-wav-read-4-bit-adpcm-at-the-rate-its-bits-make.patch) | The WAV demuxer left Yamaha ADPCM and G.722 to FFmpeg's, which seeks by the byte rate the header states.
